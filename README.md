# I am a Fluff

A short clicker-like & visual novel story game made in Godot 4.2.2, written in GDScript.  

Play as Puff, a Fluff who left Earth to travel the Milky Way in search of her Ma!  
- Collect stars for Puff  
- Help Puff grow using collected stars  
- Trade stars for items to explore further  
- Pick up mementos of Ma (optional)  
Sit back, relax, and enjoy watching the stars align with the story of Puff and her Ma.  
  
Playtime: ~20 minutes  
Endings: 3  

**Note**: since there is no save feature, please make sure you are done with your current sessions before exiting the game.


### Walkthrough  
You can get the first ending by not getting all of Ma's mementos. 3 are found in the shop (1 for each level of exploration), and the other 2 are automatically earned by exploring.  
The last two endings are by picking each choice after obtaining all of Ma's mementos.  

To get all endings in one playthrough, you should get all mementos except the last one in the shop, play the first ending, then continue on the title screen and buy the last memento. Explore again to get the options for each of the last two endings.  

Happy exploring, Puff!

- save & load files, seems like there could be some itch.io issues for web porting  
- the og telescope function was planned to be more complex, grouping stars so they could be collected quickly. its current function spawns an extra star every 4 seconds  
- an extra minigame for between explorations  
  
this unfortunately does mean the game must be completed in one playthrough and there are less interesting gamelpay components, thus i have scaled the gameplay to be quicker to compensate
