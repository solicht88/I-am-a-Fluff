# I am a Fluff

A clicker & story game made in Godot 4.2.2, written in GDScript  
Play as Puff, a Fluff who left earth to travel the Milky Way in search of her Ma  
- Collect stars for Puff  
- Help Puff grow using stars  
- Trade stars for items to explore further  
- Pick up mementos of Ma (Optional)  

### Dev Notes  
due to various personal reasons & time constraints, ive decided to omit certain features for the time being, which may or may not be added later on:  
- save & load files, seems like there could be some itch.io issues for web porting  
- the og telescope function was planned to be more complex, grouping stars so they could be collected quickly  
- an extra minigame for between explorations  
  
this unfortunately does mean the game must be completed in one playthrough, thus i have scaled the gameplay to be much quicker to compensate
